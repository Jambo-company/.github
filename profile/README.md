### Useful tools. Lasting impact.

We are **jambo.team**, a product studio working across Tanzania and Korea. We build mobile apps, web platforms and developer tools, bringing local insight and global engineering together.

[Explore our work](https://jambo.team/projects) · [About us](https://jambo.team/mission) · [Careers](https://jambo.team/careers) · [Get in touch](https://jambo.team/contact)

## Products & collaborations

| Product | What it helps you do |
| :--- | :--- |
| **[Orlim](https://jambo.team/projects/orlim)** | Keep coding-tool usage, active sessions and your Mac workspace in view. |
| **[RunEnv](https://runenv.dev/)** | Manage team secrets and deliver them to processes at runtime. |
| **[Kumbuka](https://kumbuka.biz/)** | Capture business cards, remember relationship context and follow through as a team. |
| **[HostLink](https://hostlink.io/)** | Bring your profile, links and storefront into one shareable place. |
| **[Bogoseo](https://jambo.team/projects/bogoseo)** | Connect field inspections with digital reports. |
